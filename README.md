# 🇮🇳 JanMitra AI

### Your AI Guide to Citizen Benefits & Services

JanMitra AI is an AI-powered citizen assistance platform designed to help
people discover potentially relevant government schemes, subsidies,
scholarships, welfare programmes and public services based on their
personal situation.

Instead of requiring citizens to know the name of a scheme, JanMitra
understands their context and guides them through:

**Understand → Discover → Check → Prepare → Track**

---

## 🚨 Problem

Government benefits and public services are fragmented across multiple
portals, departments and eligibility rules.

Citizens often struggle with:

- Finding relevant schemes
- Understanding eligibility criteria
- Identifying required documents
- Re-entering the same information
- Tracking applications
- Following up on pending actions

---

## 💡 Our Solution

JanMitra AI acts as a context-aware citizen assistant.

A citizen simply describes their situation.

JanMitra then:

1. Understands the citizen's context
2. Discovers potentially relevant programmes
3. Checks eligibility requirements
4. Identifies required documents
5. Prepares the next steps
6. Tracks application progress
7. Triggers follow-up actions

---

## 🤖 AI + Automation

### AI — Groq

Used for:

- Natural-language understanding
- Context extraction

- Scheme matching
- Eligibility analysis
- Document understanding

### Automation — n8n

Used for:

- Workflow orchestration
- Multi-step automation
- Notifications
- Follow-ups
- Application workflow management
- Scheduled tasks
